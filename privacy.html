Privacy policy

Claude Manager Mail is a personal application used only by its author, Wannes Luts De Martelaere, with his own Google accounts.

  • The application accesses Gmail data only for the account that signs in, in order to show, sort, translate and draft replies to that account's own mail.
  • Mail data and sign-in tokens are stored only on the author's own computer. They are not sold, shared or transferred to anyone else.
  • Mail content may be processed by an AI model on the author's behalf to sort it and draft replies; it is not used to train models and not used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
